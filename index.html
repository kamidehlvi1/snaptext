SnapText Rx · for pharmacies and clinics

Handwritten prescriptions into a checked medicine list

Snip a prescription and SnapText Rx reads each medicine line with two handwriting readers. A general one and a prescription-trained one check each other. Names are matched to the drug list and your own formulary, so a look-alike drug is never guessed.

82%right medicine first, on another hospital's prescriptions
78–87%right medicine among the choices offered
≈1%shown confidently wrong; every row is checked by a person

Measured on 1,889 handwritten medicine words from two public Bangladeshi prescription datasets, none used for training, with the pharmacy's stock list loaded. How we measured

How a prescription is checked
  1. 1Snip the prescription. Each medicine line becomes a card with the original handwriting next to it.
  2. 2A name is pre-filled only when both readers agree and it closely matches a known drug. Look-alikes are flagged for you to choose.
  3. 3Confirm each row and enter your name. Only then can you copy or export CSV/JSON.

Not a medical device. SnapText Rx is an assistant for typing prescriptions. A pharmacist or trained person must check every line against the original. Don't use it to decide what to dispense without that check.

Questions
Frequently asked

    

      
What happens after the 30-day trial?
Snaptext keeps working on the free tier with 3 scans a day, forever. A scan is one document or one capture. A licence removes the limit.

      
Does Snaptext send my documents anywhere?
No. Snaptext makes no network connections: no cloud, no telemetry, no account. You can check this with your internet switched off.

      
Why do you need my Machine ID?
Your licence is signed for one computer. The Machine ID is a one-way fingerprint of that PC and contains no personal data. Without it, we can't make a licence that activates offline.

      
I have several PCs. What do I need?
Each PC needs its own licence. Paste all the Machine IDs into the form, one per line, or choose a site licence for an organisation.

      
I'm replacing my PC or reinstalling Windows.
Reinstalling Snaptext keeps the same Machine ID. A new PC or a Windows reinstall gives a new ID. Email us the old and new IDs and we'll help you move your licence.

      
Does a Snaptext licence work in SnapText Rx?
No. They are separate products with separate licences. The Handwriting Pack is included with Snaptext.

      
Windows warns me when I run the installer.
The installers are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. You can check each file against the SHA-256 hashes on the releases page.

      
Is SnapText Rx safe to use for dispensing?
It's an assistant, not a medical device. It never silently changes a medicine name, it flags look-alike drugs, and it won't export until a person confirms every row. A pharmacist must always check the original prescription.

      
What languages does it read?
Snaptext is built for printed English text. The Handwriting Pack and SnapText Rx are trained on English handwriting.
