SnapText Rx - Intended use and safety

Please read this before installing.

WHAT SNAPTEXT RX IS
SnapText Rx is a TRANSCRIPTION AID. It reads a photo or scan of a prescription, suggests
medicine names from a drug list, and helps a trained person type up the prescription faster.

WHAT IT IS NOT
- It is not a medical device and does not make clinical decisions.
- It does not check doses, interactions, allergies or whether a prescription is appropriate.
- It does not replace a pharmacist, doctor or any other qualified person.

WHY A PERSON MUST CHECK EVERY LINE
Handwriting is often misread. Many medicine names look alike (for example Hydroxyzine and
Hydralazine, Celebrex and Celexa). SnapText Rx therefore:
- never replaces what it read without showing you,
- marks names it is unsure about and makes you choose,
- will not copy or export anything until every medicine has been confirmed by a person
  and that person's name is entered under "Checked by".

YOUR RESPONSIBILITY
By installing SnapText Rx you agree that every medicine, strength, frequency, duration and
instruction will be checked against the original prescription by a qualified person before it
is used for dispensing or any other purpose, and that you will follow the rules that apply to
your practice where you work.

PRIVACY
SnapText Rx runs entirely on this computer. Prescriptions, patient details and results are
never sent anywhere.
